How to choose a gaming headset

🎙️ Mic quality

If you play with a team, the mic matters more than the sound. Look for clear, noise-filtered voice.

😌 Comfort

Weight and cushion type decide whether you can wear it for hours. Glasses wearers, check clamp force.

🔌 Wired vs wireless

Wireless is convenient; wired means zero latency and no charging. Both are great now — pick your priority.

💵 Budget

You don't need to overspend. The sweet spot for most players is the $50–$130 range.
